About / food, rooms, music, no boring stuff

Not just the guy in the kitchen.

Skipping the BS: Tiago Borges builds the whole feeling around a table. Food, team, timing, music, and the small piece of madness that makes a room come alive.

Professional arc

Faial made the instinct. The world made the range.

Tiago comes from Faial in the Azores. No postcard story, just salt, generosity, direct flavours, and the instinct that food should feel alive before it acts clever. He trained at Escola de Formacao Turistica e Hoteleira in Sao Miguel, then got the real education at AZOR Hotel: stations, rushes, heat, mistakes, and all the things a clean recipe never teaches.

He worked around serious names, including Mark Nielson and Josh DeChellis, then joined DeChellis for the opening of Juniper Hill in New Jersey. Opening a restaurant teaches another muscle: food, people, nerves, timing, and the first night when everyone pretends they are calm.

Ibiza gave the story heat. Tiago arrived on the island with technique in his hands, music in his pockets, and the kind of curiosity that makes him say yes before the safe plan is fully written.

Bottega il Buco became a special place. Not just a restaurant on the CV, but a family Tiago kept returning to: New York roots, Italian staples, Ibiza produce, bread, focaccia, crudos, rich pastas, market plates, and years of shared service. He left, came back, helped protect the quality of the food, and stayed inseparable from the place in the way only a chef can. Recently, his collaboration with Stevie Benanty has pushed Bottega into a new level of creativity.

At WOW Ibiza, he pushed worldwide food through a head-chef lens: technique, soul, spices, comfort, and flavour combinations that refused to sit still. Later in Goa, he advised and supported the setup of Milagros, using the restaurant brain he had built in Europe in a completely different rhythm.

The new challenge is Mira-Lounge, where Tiago is realizing his love for fusion cuisine: Portuguese instinct, Ibiza appetite, world flavours, and a room that wants more than a polite dinner. Believe me, taste is not enough if the room has no pulse.

And then there is Matan Klevan. In Ibiza, Tiago found someone who heard music the way he thinks about food: balance, surprise, ingredients, love, and a little danger at the right moment. Their friendship became a collaboration, and the collaboration keeps feeding the same dream: beautiful sounds, long nights, strange discoveries, and tables that turn into stories.

Food & Music have much more in common than one would initially think, it’s all about the perfect balance of ingredients and love
