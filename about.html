About / origin, training, point of view

Island memory. Kitchen discipline. Room instinct.

Tiago's work carries the directness of island food, the discipline of serious kitchens, and the human read of someone who listens to a room before he changes it.

Professional arc

From Faial to Ibiza, without turning origin into costume.

Faial and the Azores sit in the background: memory, ingredients, sea air, and a respect for food that does not need theatre.

The current work is in Ibiza and Formentera: restaurants, openings, kitchens, consulting projects, and food-led rooms that need taste and coordination.

Tiago's role is not only to cook. It is to help the idea become repeatable: the menu, the prep, the pass, the team, the supplier logic, the room pulse, and the small details guests remember later.
