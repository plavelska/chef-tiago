About / food, music, people, no boring stuff

Not just the chef behind the pass.

Skipping the BS: this is about the whole feeling around a table. Food, team, timing, music, and the small piece of madness that makes a night come alive.

Professional arc

Faial made the instinct. The world made the range.

Faial, in the Azores, set the instinct: salt, generosity, direct flavours, and the belief that food should feel alive before it acts clever. Training began at Escola de Formacao Turistica e Hoteleira in Sao Miguel; the real education followed at AZOR Hotel: stations, rushes, heat, mistakes, and everything a clean recipe never teaches.

Serious names came next, including Mark Nielson and Josh DeChellis, followed by the opening of Juniper Hill in New Jersey alongside DeChellis. An opening builds another muscle: food, people, nerves, timing, and that first night when everyone pretends to be calm.

Ibiza gave the story heat: technique in hand, music in the pocket, and the kind of curiosity that says yes before the safe plan is fully written.

Bottega il Buco became more than a line on the CV: a family worth returning to. New York roots, Italian staples, Ibiza produce, bread, focaccia, crudos, rich pastas, market plates, and years of shared service. Leaving, returning, protecting the quality of the food, staying inseparable from the place: that is the kind of bond only a kitchen can build. More recently, the collaboration with Stevie Benanty has pushed Bottega into another level of creativity.

At WOW Ibiza, worldwide food met a head-chef lens: technique, soul, spices, comfort, and flavour combinations that refused to sit still. Goa brought a different rhythm, with advice and setup support for Milagros and a restaurant brain shaped in Europe put to work somewhere entirely new.

The new challenge is Mira-Lounge, a place for fusion cuisine: Portuguese instinct, Ibiza appetite, world flavours, and more than a polite dinner. Taste is not enough if the night has no pulse.

And then there is Matan Klevan. Ibiza brought together two ways of thinking: food and music, balance and surprise, ingredients and love, with a little danger at exactly the right moment. Friendship became collaboration, feeding the same dream of beautiful sounds, long nights, strange discoveries, and tables that turn into stories.

Food & Music have much more in common than one would initially think, it’s all about the perfect balance of ingredients and love

Matan Klevan
