Music / room rhythm

Food first. Music in the walls.

This is not a second profession page. It is the part of Tiago's hospitality brain that understands pace, warmth, lift, and the moment a table becomes a memory.

Room rhythm

Music is timing, not decoration.

A room has a tempo. Prep has one too. So does the first drink, the first plate, the hour when conversation loosens, and the last bite before people decide to stay.

Tiago treats music as part of the hospitality system: never louder than the food, never flatter than the room, always tuned to the people at the table.

Room score

A food-led night has movements.

  1. Warm-upDoors open, room settles, glasses land.
  2. First platesKeep the pace light enough for conversation.
  3. Table liftEnergy rises when the food has earned it.
  4. Late roomLess volume, more memory.

If the project is a room, a table, or a food-led night, start with the shape of it.
