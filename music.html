Sound instinct

African sounds, island trouble, no boring selections.

Music has always been there. Not as background, not as a fashionable add-on, and not because every creative person suddenly needs a DJ sentence. It is its own thing: a private obsession, a social passport, and a way to follow people, places, and late-night instinct.

His ears keep pulling him toward African sounds: funky, percussive, warm, strange in the best way, and built for bodies before algorithms. It is music with dust on its shoes and sunlight in its bones. The kind of sound that makes people move before they ask what it is.

In 2025, half a year in Zanzibar led to whynot.club.zanzibar. The island got under his skin fast: the pace, the people, the sea air, the records, the drums, and the feeling that the night could keep unfolding if nobody interrupted it with a plan.

For a moment, Zanzibar almost won. The island and its sound made putting down roots there feel like a real thought, not a holiday fantasy. In the end, Ibiza won the battle of the islands. But the African sound stayed: loose, alive, generous, never flat.
