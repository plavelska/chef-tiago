Selected projects

Where the skills got tested.

01 / Mira

The new challenge: fusion with a pulse.

Portuguese instinct, Ibiza appetite, world flavours.

Mira Lounge Ibiza is the new challenge: a room where Tiago is realizing his love for fusion cuisine through Portuguese instinct, Ibiza appetite, world flavours, and a service rhythm that wants more than a polite dinner.

His role is concept and kitchen leadership: shaping the food direction, building rhythm with the team, and making sure the menu, the room, and the atmosphere feel like one idea.

02 / Bottega

A long love story with a serious room.

Simple food, exact hands, real family.

Bottega il Buco is not just a line on Tiago's CV. He was part of the Ibiza story around the New York il Buco family: local producers, Italian staples, bread, focaccia, crudos, pastas, market plates, desserts, and a room with soul.

Tiago's role moved through the real stuff: keeping food simple but exact, protecting quality, understanding the team, and returning to the Bottega family when the food needed his hands again. His recent collaboration with Stevie Benanty has helped push the place into a more creative chapter.

03 / Milagros

Spanish coastline, Goan cliff, real setup work.

Beauty made operational.

In Goa, Tiago worked with chef Silvia Grimaldo while supporting the setup of Milagros, known publicly as Milagro Goa: a Small/Ozran Vagator restaurant with direct beach access, sunset views, cocktails, and a coastal Spanish spirit.

The place had beauty already: a cliffside mood, a menu remembered for dishes like lobster spaghetti and calamari salad, and Silvia's elegant Spanish hand. Tiago's role was to help make that beauty operational: kitchen structure, prep logic, service flow, team rhythm, and the practical systems that let a restaurant feel relaxed without becoming loose.

04 / WOW

Technique, soul, and flavour that refused to sit still.

An eclectic room needed a food language.

At WOW Ibiza, Tiago was head chef. The brief in his CV is exact: worldwide food, techniques from Michelin-star kitchens, the comfort of soul food, and flavour combinations with real appetite.

That made the role bigger than running a pass. WOW sat between San Rafael and Santa Gertrudis as a restaurant, cocktail bar, garden hangout, concept-store world, and social room with music in the air. Tiago's job was to give that eclectic energy a food language: local produce, global ingredients, brasserie confidence, clean plating, steady workflow, and dishes bold enough to belong to the night.
