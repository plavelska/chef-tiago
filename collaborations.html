Fit filter

Best when the project has appetite and honesty.

Good fit

Restaurant owners, hospitality operators, creative producers, music people, and friends-of-friends who want food, team, timing, sound, and atmosphere treated as one system.

Not the fit

A generic menu request, a concept with no point of view, a producer hiding the real budget, or a table where food is only decoration after the poster is finished.

What matters in the mix

Good products, clean kitchens, direct people, local intelligence, playful taste, music with feeling, and collaborators who care about the guest's whole night.
