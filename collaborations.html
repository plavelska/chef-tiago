Collaborations / selected projects, taste, timing, good trouble

Let's cook something memorable.

Best for projects with a point of view: a place to open, a menu to sharpen, a team to organize, a night to build, or a food and music idea that needs taste, timing, and nerve.

Fit filter

Best when the project has appetite and honesty.

Right kind of project

Restaurant owners, hospitality operators, creative producers, music people, and friends-of-friends who want food, team, timing, sound, and atmosphere treated as one system.

Wrong kind of project

A generic menu request, a concept with no point of view, a producer hiding the real budget, or a table where food is only decoration after the poster is finished.

What the mix needs

Good products, clean kitchens, direct people, local intelligence, playful taste, music with feeling, and collaborators who care about the guest's whole night.

What to send first

Send the rough version: what you are building, where and when it happens, who is involved, what already exists, what is missing, and what the night should feel like.

Selected collaboration lines

The projects that make sense.

01

Restaurant openings + relaunches

New openings, second chances, kitchen resets, and teams that need the food idea, prep logic, and service rhythm aligned before opening night.

02

Menu concepts + food direction

Menus with identity: seasonal, local, global when useful, realistic to execute, and strong enough to be remembered.

03

Kitchen coordination

Structure without killing service: prep systems, handover logic, sanitation, team culture, calm execution, and better communication.

04

Extraordinary events

Dinners, launches, private tables, island nights, brand moments, and beautiful gatherings where food becomes the center of gravity.

05

Pop-ups + residencies

Short runs with a clear concept, tight prep, a real host, and enough visual texture to live beyond the calendar.

06

Food + music moments

Listening dinners, after-hours tables, record-led gatherings, and nights where sound becomes part of hospitality timing.
